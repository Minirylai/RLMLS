# Data attribution and terms

The Type I, Type II, UDG and NDR benchmarks originate from the sources identified in `SOURCES.md` and the per-graph source links. Their original attribution and applicable terms remain in force. This repository does not relicense those third-party materials.

Census inputs are derived from the U.S. Census Bureau's public population-center data; the source files and geographic identifiers are retained in the provenance archive. ER, BA and SBM graphs were generated for this study, with model parameters and seeds supplied.

No repository-wide license is asserted over the combined third-party datasets. Cite the original sources when using the corresponding instances, and retain their notices when redistributing them. Original author documentation is included in the NDR provenance archive.
